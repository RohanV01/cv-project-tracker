Execution options

Two Ways to Engage

Either way, everything stays 100% on-prem at CiVentiChem. What changes is who does the data prep and who sources the hardware.

Model A

Client-Led

CiVentiChem drives infrastructure & data prep

  • CiVentiChem sources and procures all hardware (server, GPU, storage)
  • CiVentiChem's own team preps and structures the historical archive, guided by our templates
  • We build, deploy, and configure the software layer on top
Best for: teams with in-house IT bandwidth to run hardware procurement and data prep internally.
Model B

Managed, Full-Service

We drive infrastructure & data prep

  • We deploy a dedicated data team (e.g. in Hyderabad) to prep and structure the archive
  • We specify the exact hardware required; CiVentiChem procures against our spec
  • We build, deploy, and configure the software layer end-to-end
Best for: teams that want a single point of accountability and minimal internal lift.
Both models are 100% on-prem. The data and hardware always live at CiVentiChem, only who performs the prep work changes.
Beyond this roadmap

Standing Constraint & What's Not in Scope Yet

One requirement runs through every phase above; a few things are deliberately left for later.

Everything (the application, the archive, and the AI models) runs on hardware CiVentiChem owns and controls. No cloud database, no third-party LLM API, no telemetry, no data egress at any point.

Local-only technology stack

  • Local LLM runtime, on-disk vector store, local embeddings
  • SQLite / local DB, scheduled local backup, zero telemetry

Deliberately out of scope, for now

  • Commercial terms & pricing, a separate conversation
  • Ongoing support/maintenance retainer, proposed once live
